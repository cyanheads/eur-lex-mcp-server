# 0.18.0 — 2026-09-25

Breaking for callers that read these values:

- `eurlex_get_cases` omits `title` and the `**Full title:**` text line when the parse is complete (about 98% of rows). "Joined Cases" rows now fill `case_reference`, so their `subject_matter` changes too.
- Case law in `eurlex_get_document` reports the parties as `title` instead of the raw `#`-joined string. Its outline lists `heading` and `operative_part` entries, and `select.articles` on case law lands in `selection.missed`.
- Author labels are CELLAR's English prefLabels, e.g. "Council of the EU" is now "Council of the European Union".
- A non-English `eurlex_get_document` request returns its `title` in the served language.
- Relation text rows gain a ` · date · title` suffix.

## Added

- **`formation`, `referring_court`, and `advocate_general` in `eurlex_get_cases`**, parsed from the CELLAR title by `parseCaseLawTitle`, which `eurlex_get_document` shares ([#116](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/116)).
- **Case-law metadata in `eurlex_get_document`**: `ecli` (by the `eurlex_lookup_celex` rule), plus `formation`, `referring_court`, `subject_matter`, and `case_reference` from the title ([#117](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/117)).
- **Case-law `outline` and `select`** in `eurlex_get_document`: each top-level section heading as a `heading` entry numbered by position, and a judgment's or order's ruling as one `operative_part` entry. `select.headings` and `select.operative_part` return them. The outline is the same in html, markdown, and xml ([#117](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/117)).
- **`related_date` and `related_title`** on `eurlex_get_relations` and `eurlex://document/{celexNumber}/relations` rows: the date the page is ordered by, and the English title, each omitted when the work has none ([#119](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/119)).

## Changed

- **`eurlex_get_cases` `title`** is returned only when the parsed fields miss part of it: an unplaced segment, an "(Extracts)" marker, or a title date that differs from `date`. The text channel drops the `Parties` line when it repeats the heading, "Joined Case(s) …" references parse into `case_reference`, and a title that names no parties reads its referral as `referring_court` rather than `parties` ([#116](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/116)).
- **Case-law `title` in `eurlex_get_document`** is the parties (or the court/AG descriptor) on a complete parse, else the raw English title. Case law is titled in English in every language ([#117](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/117)).

## Fixed

- **Authority-code authors** in `author_institution(s)` of `eurlex_get_document` and `eurlex://document` render as their English `skos:prefLabel` (corporate bodies, member states, MEPs) instead of raw codes such as `EURUN` or `NLD`. Each label works as the `author_institution` filter of `eurlex_search_documents` ([#103](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/103)).
- **`eurlex_get_document` `title`** follows the served language, falling back to English when CELLAR has no title in it ([#133](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/133)).
- **OJ C `keyword` fragments** (`2024/01469`, `C/2024/0146`) are answered from the CELEX full-text index on their number word: 0.5 s instead of 17.5 s. A number under four characters (`2017/111`) still scans ([#137](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/137)).
- **A parsed `case_number`** in `eurlex_get_cases` narrows through CELEX index prefix terms before its `REGEX`. `C-443/14` and `82/85`, which could pass the 55 s client timeout, answer in 0.3 s ([#138](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/138)).
