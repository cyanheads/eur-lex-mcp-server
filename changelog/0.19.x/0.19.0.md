# 0.19.0 — 2026-10-14

## Added

- **`eurlex_get_relations` relation types `basis_for`, `interpreted_by`, `interprets`** — the works adopted under an act, the CJEU rulings interpreting it, and what a ruling interprets. Omitting `relation_types` traverses all thirteen types, and `eurlex://document/{celexNumber}/relations` summarizes them (#148).
- **`eurlex_get_relations` `member_states`** narrows `national_transposition` to the named states (alpha-3 or alpha-2 codes, any case, echoed as alpha-3). It fails as `member_states_not_applicable` when `relation_types` excludes `national_transposition` (#149).
- **Pasted identifier forms resolve to their work** — a `CELEX:` prefix or lowercase CELEX, EUR-Lex page URLs carrying `uri=CELEX:…`, `publications.europa.eu/resource/celex/…` URIs, https and language-suffixed ELIs, and https work URIs — on every CELEX, ELI, and work-URI input and both document resources. A prefixed case number in `eurlex_lookup_celex` returns `found: false` pointing at `eurlex_get_cases` (#147).
- **Enum inputs accept case and separator variants** (`PAGED`, `Metadata_Only`, `metadata-only`), and `content_mode` accepts `metadata`. `tools/list` is unchanged (#143).
- **`limit_clamp` enrichment** — a `limit` above a tool's per-call maximum is served at the maximum and reported as `{ requested, applied }` on both surfaces instead of rejected. Row limits accept up to 10,000; the `eurlex_get_document` `limit` has no schema maximum (#151).

## Changed

- **`sparql_error` and `sparql_timeout` are declared on every tool and both resources**, each with its own recovery text; `sparql_error` is a `ValidationError`. Both resources also declare `not_found`, so its error carries a reason and recovery hint (#145).
- **Tool-argument pre-validation repairs more representation mistakes** (`cyanheads/mcp-ts-core` 0.13.14): numeric and boolean strings, a lone string sent for an array, and `null` at an optional key, including inside `z.preprocess` output. The `-32602` rejection is bounded, and prompt arguments are parsed once.
- **MCP SDK `@modelcontextprotocol/server` ^2.2.0 → ^2.3.1** through the framework (`cyanheads/mcp-ts-core` 0.13.15): `prompts/get` without `arguments` is validated as `{}`. Every `ZodError`'s `data.issues` is bounded and logs as a one-line summary.
- **Bun 1.4.2 → 1.4.3** in `packageManager` and the `Dockerfile` (`cyanheads/mcp-ts-core` 0.13.15). Framework skills resynced to 0.13.15.

## Fixed

- **`offset` of 10,000 or more fails as `offset_past_window`** instead of an HTTP 500 outage, and a page reaching row 10,000 serves its rows, omits `next_offset`, and carries a notice with the route past it: the closed `date_from`/`date_to` window that continues `eurlex_search_documents` and `eurlex_get_cases`, a ready-to-run `eurlex_query_sparql` query per relation type and direction on `eurlex_get_relations`, and a more specific keyword on `eurlex_browse_subjects` (#154).
- **Deterministic CELLAR errors fail at once** — a compile or sort-window error returned as HTTP 500 is a non-retryable `sparql_error` carrying CELLAR's message, not four retries ending in an outage (#145).
- **`eurlex_search_documents` with a title keyword, `author_institution`, and a date bound** no longer fails with HTTP 500 (#144).
- **Searches bounded by `date_to` alone** no longer skip in-range rows in `eurlex_search_documents` and `eurlex_get_cases` (#159).
- **`eurlex_get_cases` row fields** — title, parsed fields, types, and ECLI — come from the CELEX's own work, matching `eurlex_get_document` (#146).
- **Act structure** — `select` ends the last article, section, and chapter at the act's final part (#153), `select.annexes: "ANNEX"` reaches a lone unnumbered annex (#156), and headings inside annexes stay out of the outline (#157).
- **`eurlex_get_document` returns corrigendum bodies** (`R(NN)` CELEX numbers) (#155).
- **`eurlex_get_document` `work_uri` miss** says the work is not in CELLAR unless it exists without a CELEX; expression and manifestation URIs are served as their work (#158).
- **`eurlex_query_sparql` truncation notice** describes the `ORDER BY` plus `OFFSET` continuation instead of a cap no input raises (#150).
- **`eurlex_comparative_analysis` step 1** restates the domain in EU legal vocabulary before the EuroVoc search, and step 2 also searches by title phrase (#152).
