# 0.16.0 — 2026-09-25

## Added

- **`include_recitals`** on `eurlex_get_document`: lists every recital as its own outline entry, as before this release ([#118](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/118)).

## Changed

- **Default outline collapses recitals** into one entry per run, `{ kind: "recital", number: "1–173", label: "Recitals 1–173" }` at the first recital's offset; a lone recital keeps its own entry, and `select` still reaches any single recital. Breaking: pass `include_recitals: true` for per-recital entries, and expect a recital `number` to be a range ([#118](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/118)).
- **Outline and selection labels are English in every language and format** (`Article 4`, `CHAPTER IV`), with language-neutral numbers. Breaking: xml article labels no longer carry the native heading text (`Article premier`, `4. cikk` → `Article 1`, `Article 4`) ([#107](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/107)).
- **Quoted amending headings are not the act's own.** Breaking: they no longer appear in outlines or resolve in `select`, and a number found only in quoted text is reported in `selection.missed`; html outlines also drop headings inside tables other than recitals, such as a treaty's table of contents or a quote inside a judgment ([#106](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/106)).
- **`select` and `limit` descriptions** state that `select` ignores `offset`/`limit` and caps the joined sections at 100,000 characters ([#118](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/118)).

## Fixed

- **Heading detection in all 24 EU languages** for `outline` and `select`, through a per-language vocabulary keyed by the language served: number-first forms (`1. cikk`, `I LUKU`), trailing periods, `1.º`, French `premier`, and Greek `Ι`/`Χ` numerals. Formex articles are numbered from `<ARTICLE IDENTIFIER>`, and a selector may use the served language's kind word (`Artikel 2`, `4. cikk`) ([#107](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/107)).
- **Amending articles span their quoted text** in every format: `32015R2120` Article 7 runs to Article 8 (36,041 characters in xml) instead of stopping at its first quoted heading (6,323). Markdown takes each heading's verdict from the source HTML, which the content service now carries behind a Markdown body ([#106](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/106)).
