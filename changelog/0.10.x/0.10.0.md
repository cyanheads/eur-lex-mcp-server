# 0.10.0 — 2026-08-30

## Changed

- **`eurlex_get_document` content retrieval** now caps oversized `content_mode: "full"` responses at 100,000 characters and supplies continuation metadata; callers that assumed unbounded full content must continue with `content_mode: "paged"` until `has_more` is false (#68).
- **`eurlex_get_document` content responses** now normalize the 24 EUR-Lex language codes, distinguish ordinary content unavailability from challenges, preserve HTML/XML body source literally in `content[]`, and accept CELLAR work URIs (#74, #75).
- **`eurlex_query_sparql` Markdown tables** now escape literal lexical content without changing RDF term delimiters, datatype suffixes, or language tags (#76).
