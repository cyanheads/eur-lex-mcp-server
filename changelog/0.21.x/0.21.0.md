# 0.21.0 — 2026-11-17

One class of call is breaking: a typed `eurlex_search_documents` with no date bound and a `keyword` or `author_institution` of hundreds of non-ASCII characters is now refused as `request_too_large`. Shorten `keyword` or `author_institution`, or drop `document_type` or another filter. A date bound does not help.

## Added

- **`eurlex_lookup_celex` resolves a treaty-article citation** under `identifier_type: "auto"`: `Article 102 TFEU` → `12016E102`, `Article 4(3) TEU` → `12016M004`, `Article 47 of the Charter` → `12016P047`. An article the treaty lacks returns `found: false`; several articles, text after the treaty, and pre-Lisbon numbering (`Art 82 EC`) stay `ambiguous_identifier` ([#207](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/207)).
- **`eurlex://document/{celexNumber}` and its `/relations` resource read a percent-encoded CELEX**: `eurlex://document/12016E%2FTXT` reads `12016E/TXT`, through `createApp` `decodeResourceParams` ([#210](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/210)).

## Changed

- **Breaking: a typed `eurlex_search_documents` with no date bound has less room for `keyword`, `author_institution`, and its other filters** ([#204](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/204), [#205](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/205)). Its family clause is 707–1,547 bytes longer, so a `PROP` search with `include_consolidated` and a 700-character Greek `keyword`, a 7,642-byte request in 0.20.3, is now 8,676 bytes and refused unsent. A search with `date_from` or `date_to`, or with no `document_type`, is not narrowed.
- **A request CELLAR answers with HTTP 403 is sent once**, not four times ([#206](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/206)).
- **The `sparql_timeout` recovery of `eurlex_search_documents` and `eurlex_get_cases`** says a partial CELEX `keyword` that opens with type letters needs more of its number (`R0679`, not `R0`) ([#208](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/208)).
- **A miss on a treaty-article CELEX with a one- or two-digit article number names the three-digit CELEX CELLAR holds** (`12016E35` → `12016E035`).
- **A dated `eurlex_search_documents` sends its match once**, so it has more room for `keyword`: 2,950 letters for `PROP` with `include_consolidated`, up from 574.
- **`eurlex_comparative_analysis` plan step 6** resolves a treaty article's citation with `eurlex_lookup_celex` ([#207](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/207)).
- **`eur-lex-guide` skill 1.3 → 1.4**, covering treaty-article citations and the request limit.

## Fixed

- **`eurlex_search_documents` rows and `document_type` matching read the work a CELEX resolves to.** For a CELEX held by several works, a row's `resource_type` and `title` can differ from 0.20.3's, and four rows leave filtered searches: `51987DC0290` (`COMMUNIC`), `51987DC0452`, `51988DC0239`, and `51988DC0400` (`REPORT`) ([#204](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/204)).
- **`TREATY` covers the Treaties' protocols and declarations**, and `AGREE` covers agreement protocols ([#205](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/205)).
- **A request over CELLAR's 8,192-byte limit fails as `request_too_large` without being sent**, where it failed as a service error after four attempts. Every tool but `eurlex_get_relations`, and `eurlex://document/{celexNumber}`, declare the reason ([#206](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/206)).
- **CELEX keywords stay within the request limit** in `eurlex_search_documents` and `eurlex_get_cases`: a whole-CELEX family lookup splits across requests, and a type-letter fragment such as `R0679` resolves its terms by lookups first, then answers ([#208](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/208)).
- **`eurlex_get_relations` returns a related CELEX held by several works once**, from the work it resolves to, with `limit`, `offset`, and `has_more` counting those rows; the relations resource lists it once too ([#209](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/209)).
