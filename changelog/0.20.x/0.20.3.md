# 0.20.3 — 2026-11-14

## Added

- **`eurlex_search_documents` `document_type` adds `COMMUNIC`, `REPORT`, `SWD`, and `AGREE`**: Commission communications, reports, staff working documents (with their impact assessments and evaluations), and international agreements ([#201](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/201)).
- **`eurlex_lookup_celex` returns the resolved work's English `title`** on every found path, verbatim and rendered in `content[]` after the type and date ([#202](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/202)).
- **`eurlex_comparative_analysis` plan adds two EU case-law steps**: `eurlex_get_cases` by case-law `subject`, then `eurlex_get_relations` `interpreted_by` on the key acts, their legal-basis treaty articles, and Articles 101 and 102 TFEU ([#203](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/203)).

## Changed

- **`eur-lex-guide` skill 1.2 → 1.3**, covering treaty article versions and `recorded_against`, the notice-only case-number answer, and the treaty `article` filter.
- `@biomejs/biome` 2.6.6 → 2.6.7 (dev).

## Fixed

- **`eurlex_lookup_celex`, `eurlex_get_document`, and `eurlex://document/{celexNumber}` report every resource type of a work** CELLAR files under several (`Corrigendum, Regulation`), as search and case rows do ([#197](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/197)). `eurlex_get_document` and the resource also read the English title when CELLAR records an empty title literal beside it (`12020W/PRO/01`).
- **`eurlex_get_relations` on a TFEU, TEU, or Charter article or text reads the incoming edges of all four Lisbon versions as one list**, one row per related work, newest first; `recorded_against` on each row names the CELEX its edge targets, so `interpreted_by` on `12016E102` returns its rulings ([#199](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/199)). `article` on a treaty whole text lists that article's rulings across versions; on a treaty article CELEX, its own article applies no filter and another article returns an empty page with a notice.
- **`eurlex_get_relations` provisions follow-up splits its requests under CELLAR's 8,192-byte request limit** (`interpreted_by` at `limit` 100 on `32016R0679` returned no provisions), and a type named twice in `relation_types` is queried and echoed once.
- **`eurlex_get_cases` on a case number CELLAR files only notices under returns them** with a `notice` and `query_echo.include_derivative: true`, covering cases lodged and not yet decided and cases closed by an order published only as a notice ([#200](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/200)). A `court` naming the number's own court no longer stops this, and an empty page's notice no longer offers removing it.
- **`eurlex_get_cases` calls at `limit` above 70, and `subject` phrases matching many subjects (`"e"`), stay under CELLAR's 8,192-byte request limit** (they failed with HTTP 403).
