# 0.13.1 — 2026-09-25

## Changed

- **`eurlex_get_cases` with a `date_from` or `date_to`** selects the page's CELEX keys before aggregating titles, types, and ECLI; rows, order, `has_more`, and `next_offset` match the flat query, and a one-month range dropped from 9.5–46 s to 0.35–1.0 s in paired runs ([#98](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/98)).
- **Framework sync** — `framework-skills/`, `CLAUDE.md`/`AGENTS.md`, `.env.example` (per-signal `OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_*_ENDPOINT`), and the feature-request template follow mcp-ts-core 0.13.7; `package.json` `files` excludes built `dist/*.mcpb` bundles.

## Fixed

- **Canonical work for a multi-work CELEX** — a CELEX CELLAR holds under several works resolves to the one `owl:sameAs` its `…/resource/celex/{CELEX}` IRI, else the lowest work URI, in `eurlex_lookup_celex` (CELEX and ECLI), `eurlex_get_document`, `eurlex_get_relations`, both `eurlex://document` resources, and the `work_uri` of `eurlex_get_cases` and `eurlex_search_documents` rows. A `work_uri` naming a copy is served as the resolved work, and `eurlex_lookup_celex` reports the case's ECLI when only a copy records it ([#97](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/97)).
- **`advocates_general`** — `eurlex_get_document` and `eurlex://document/{celexNumber}` list a case-law record's Advocates General by surname, and `author_institution(s)` no longer carry their CELLAR UUIDs; a national-court decision's author is the court's name. An AG opinion whose only recorded author is the AG has no `author_institution` ([#96](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/96)).
- **Relation pages** in `eurlex_get_relations` and `eurlex://document/{celexNumber}/relations` are the newest related works, identical on every call, with consecutive offsets that neither overlap nor skip; ties break on the work URI and undated works sort last ([#100](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/100)).
- **Page order in `eurlex_get_cases` and `eurlex_search_documents`** breaks date ties on the CELEX, so identical calls return identical pages and paging never repeats or skips a record that shares its date ([#102](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/102)).
- **Formex quotation marks** — `QUOT.START`/`QUOT.END` elements in `format: "xml"` heading titles render as the character their `CODE` names, so GDPR's English XML outline matches its HTML outline on all 298 headings ([#99](https://github.com/cyanheads/eur-lex-mcp-server/issues/99)).
- **Label lookups** — `RESOURCE_TYPE_LABELS` and `CORPORATE_BODY_LABELS` are `Map`s, so a URI such as `constructor` falls back to its last path segment instead of resolving through the prototype chain.
- **Cancelled content fetches** — a EUR-Lex fetch that fails after the request was cancelled is rethrown as a cancellation rather than reported as `upstream_failure`.
